## 🧐 What is CredMail?
CredMail is a lightweight browser extension that acts as your personal security analyst. It spots phishing emails, dangerous links, and typosquatting attacks directly inside Gmail—without invading your privacy.

Unlike other antivirus tools, **CredMail runs 100% locally on your device.** Your emails, passwords, and browsing history are never sent to the cloud.

---

## 📸 See it in Action

### 1. Smart Inbox Scanning
CredMail scans your inbox list passively. It flags emails with urgent language ("Act Now!"), brand impersonation, or suspicious sender names before you even open them.
<img width="1547" height="128" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/65f70270-80d3-452b-9569-b7efd15a34b4" />


### 2. Deep Link Analysis
When you open an email, CredMail analyzes every link for hidden threats:
*   **Typosquatting:** Detects fake domains (e.g., `paypaal.in` vs `paypal.com`).
*   **Homoglyph Attacks:** Spots fake characters (e.g., Cyrillic 'a' vs Latin 'a').
*   **Hidden Redirects:** Warns you if a link claims to be one site but takes you to another.

### 3. User Control & Dashboard
You are in control. Accidentally flagged a friend? Whitelist them instantly. Found a phishing email? Report it to your local blocklist. Manage everything from a clean dashboard.

## ✨ Key Features

*   **The Protector:** If you accidentally click a bad link, CredMail monitors the destination page. If a suspicious site asks for your password, CredMail **pauses the submission** and warns you immediately.
<img width="1105" height="774" alt="Screenshot 2025-12-30 102827" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/31b0b344-50b4-4f03-8394-76afc89d3a6d" />

*   **Zero-Day Detection:** Doesn't rely on slow databases. Uses real-time logic to catch new attacks the moment they are created.
*   **No "Alert Fatigue":** Low-risk emails get a subtle "Safety Check" badge. Only high-risk threats trigger the red alert.

## 🔒 Privacy Policy
**We do not collect your data.**

*   **No Cloud:** All analysis happens in your browser using JavaScript.
*   **No Tracking:** We do not use Google Analytics or tracking pixels.
*   **Local Storage:** Your whitelist, blacklist, and incident logs are stored in `chrome.storage.local` on your own machine.
